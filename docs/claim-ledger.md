# Material claim ledger

This ledger separates observed technical claims from assumptions, limitations, and future
recommendations. Each claim is limited to the recorded synthetic lab configuration. The governing
summary is the [`bounded assurance statement`](assurance-statement.md).

| Claim | Observed statement | Verifiable evidence | Boundary or limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| CLAIM-001 | The mandatory evaluation path is deterministic, local, and requires no API key, SaaS call, paid call, or model download after locked dependencies are available. | [`Makefile`](../Makefile), [`uv.lock`](../uv.lock), deterministic provider in [`application.py`](../src/mlflow_ai_assurance_lab/application.py), and the executable checks in [`tests/`](../tests/) | Dependency acquisition is a setup prerequisite. This claim does not cover an optional provider, hosted service, or production deployment. |
| CLAIM-002 | The committed sample contains eight distinct case/trace anchors per variant and records distinguishable `baseline` and `governed` observations. | [`summary.json`](../evidence/sample/summary.json), [`case-results.json`](../evidence/sample/case-results.json), and `CTRL-TRACE-001` in [`control-status.json`](../evidence/sample/control-status.json) | MLflow identifiers are local and volatile. Trace coverage does not establish production observability or trace completeness outside these cases. |
| CLAIM-003 | In the committed sample, baseline scored `0.25`, `0.875`, `0.875`, `0.375`, and `0.25`, producing `FAIL`; governed scored `1.0` on all five required metrics, producing `PASS`. | [`summary.json`](../evidence/sample/summary.json), [`report.md`](../evidence/sample/report.md), and threshold binding in [`manifest.json`](../evidence/sample/manifest.json) | The comparison covers eight synthetic fixtures and five bounded matching metrics. It does not establish semantic truth, factual accuracy, real-model quality, or broader risk coverage. |
| CLAIM-004 | The gate recomputes decisions and rejects a measured regression or invalid, missing, non-finite, out-of-range, stale, or policy-inconsistent inputs without a success fallback. | `CTRL-GATE-001` in [`control-status.json`](../evidence/sample/control-status.json), implementation in [`gate.py`](../src/mlflow_ai_assurance_lab/gate.py), and negative tests in [`test_gate.py`](../tests/test_gate.py) | The gate is only as complete as the versioned metrics, cases, and policy. Human oversight remains required. |
| CLAIM-005 | For the executed sample, configured synthetic markers, credential shapes, and prohibited prompt phrases were scoreable before persistence and absent from inspected persisted traces/evidence. | `CTRL-REDACT-001` in [`control-status.json`](../evidence/sample/control-status.json), persistence flags in [`summary.json`](../evidence/sample/summary.json), and integration tests in [`test_tracing.py`](../tests/test_tracing.py) | `persistence-redaction-v2` is configured pattern matching, not general PII, secret, prompt-injection, or exfiltration detection. |
| CLAIM-006 | The evidence validator binds schemas, current source/input digests, complete case coverage, recomputed decisions and control observations, safe content, material digest, and generated-file hashes. | [`manifest.json`](../evidence/sample/manifest.json), `CTRL-EVID-001` in [`control-status.json`](../evidence/sample/control-status.json), contract in [`evidence-provenance.md`](evidence-provenance.md), and negative tests in [`test_evidence.py`](../tests/test_evidence.py) | Hash linkage does not establish author identity, chain of custody, independent time, immutability, or complete audit readiness. |
| CLAIM-007 | Every registered material risk has bidirectional links to versioned controls, implementation/configuration, tests, measured anchors or evidence-level observations, decisions, and residual risk. | [`risk-assessment.md`](risk-assessment.md), generated [`control-matrix.md`](control-matrix.md), machine-readable [`control-status.json`](../evidence/sample/control-status.json), and catalog [`control-catalog-v1.json`](../config/control-catalog-v1.json) | Link completeness is not control effectiveness beyond the measured fixtures and does not replace independent assurance. |
| CLAIM-008 | The NIST, ISO/IEC, OWASP, MITRE ATLAS, and EU entries are educational architecture or threat-vocabulary crosswalks with source versions and explicit limits. | [`standards-crosswalk.md`](standards-crosswalk.md), [`threat-model.md`](threat-model.md), and [`source-register.md`](source-register.md) | These mappings do not establish legal applicability, compliance, conformity, certification, framework alignment, or comprehensive threat coverage. |

Recommendations for enterprise extension are intentionally not claims. They are recorded separately
in [`findings.md`](findings.md).
